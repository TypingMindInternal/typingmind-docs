You can now sort your chat history on the left side panel!

## 🔥 What’s new?

Easily organize your chats by:

- **Recent Chats First**
- **Oldest Chats First**
- **Title A to Z**
- **Title Z to A**

Just click the sort icon in your left-side panel to choose your preference.
