# Transfer Ownership

### **🚀 What's New?**

New in [custom.typingmind.com](http://custom.typingmind.com/): **Transfer ownership**

You can now easily transfer the ownership of your chat instance to another team member.

***Note***: Before you can set a member as the new owner, they must first be elevated to an Admin role.
