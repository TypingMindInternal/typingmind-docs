# Suspend members

### **🚀 What's New?**

New in [custom.typingmind.com](http://custom.typingmind.com/): Suspend members🛑

Members who are suspended:

- Cannot access the chat instance, but their data will be retained.
- Do not count towards the total number of seats.
- Can be unsuspended at any time.
