## Roles

To avoid confusion between parties involved in this partnership, we will agree on the following terms:

- **Software platform provider**: this is us, TypingMind (company name: Devuap LLC), which can also be referred to as “technical partner” or “software manufacturer”. We build and maintain the software, push out updates, handle data, security, and everything involved in the technical platform.
- **Reseller/Agency**: this is you. You help implement TypingMind to businesses in your local market and provide added-value services like AI consulting, training, workshops about AI for the businesses on top of the TypingMind platform.
- **Customers**: the companies or universities who will pay for the product and service.
- **End-users**: employees or students who will use the TypingMind platform. Some end-users may get elected to Admin roles where they can manage the TypingMind Admin Panel. ## Choose Your Reselling Structure

The reselling structure can be flexible. Depends on the level of engagement you want to be involved in. Here are the factors:

### **1. Resell Type: Seat vs. Instance**

Choose which type of reselling you want to go with.

**Seat resell** means you create one instance under your brand and then sell each seat to your customers. Your customers can only access the end-user interface, not the Admin panel. Each user will be one seat.

**Instance resell** means you create a private instance for each of your customers under their brand name, then you help them set up and manage the instance. Optionally, you can give them access to the Admin panel and let them customize the instance for their company if they prefer.

### **2. AI Providers and API Cost**

Choose who will pay for the AI providers.

**Option 1 – Reseller pay (Recommended)**: you pay for the AI providers and use your API key to set up the instance for your customer. This means you will need an account at OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google AI.

**Option 2 – Customer pay**: you only help the customer set up the instance, then the customer will use their own API keys and pay the AI providers directly.

### 3. Deployment

Choose where to deploy your instance. TypingMind supports deployments in the following ways:

- **Cloud host (recommended)**: our cloud environment is hosted in two data centers: the US (Virginia) and EU (Frankfurt). Customers can choose to host their data on our cloud host for maximum convenience and enjoy seamless software updates
- **Self-host on customer’s infrastructure**: TypingMind can be deployed to the customers’ infrastructure. This requires a technical team to set up and maintain continuously. The self-host version comes with much more limitations. More information about the self-host version is available at [https://custom.typingmind.com/self-host](https://custom.typingmind.com/self-host)
- **Self-host on your infrastructure:** You can host TypingMind privately on your infrastructure and then provide access to your customers from there. In this model, you have the responsibility to host and protect your customers’ data. You will need a technical team to set up and maintain the self-host system.

We always recommend that customers host their instances in our cloud environment when possible. It is easier to deliver software updates and provide customer support on our cloud. We have industry-standard security and privacy policies to ensure customers’ data is safe.

### 4. Value-added services for your customers

TypingMind acts as a technical partner, empowering resellers to deliver more than just the platform. We encourage you to create additional value for your customers by offering services such as:

- Assisting with the creation of AI Agents, prompts, and custom workflows
- Bundling the platform with your own consulting or training services
- Providing tailored solutions or support based on specific customer needs

This not only enhances the customer experience but also helps you differentiate your offering and grow your business.
