TypingMind lets you access and manage multiple AI models from different providers in one unified workspace.

## Official Model Support

TypingMind officially supports LLMs from 12 AI providers, including:

1. [OpenAI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/openai-\(gpt-5-gpt-4.1\)): GPT models
2. [Anthropic Claude](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/anthropic-claude): Claude models
3. [Google Gemini](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/google-gemini-\(gemini-3.1-nano-banana\)): Gemini models
4. [OpenRouter](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/openrouter): models from multiple providers
5. [DeepInfra](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/deepinfra): open-source models such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek
6. [DeepSeek](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/deepseek-ai): DeepSeek models
7. [Groq](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/groq-api-\(llama-3-mixtral-8x7b-gemma-7b\)): fast inference models such as Llama, Qwen
8. [Mistral](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/mistral-ai): Mistral models
9. [Moonshot](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/moonshot-ai): Kimi models
10. [Perplexity](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/perplexity-ai): Sonar models
11. [xAI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/xai-\(grok-ai\)): Grok models
12. [Z.ai](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/z.ai): GLM models

## Custom Models

If your models/providers are not available in the official support list, you can add custom models on TypingMind, here are some examples:

1. [Novita AI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/novita-ai): open-source models such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek
2. [Azure OpenAI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/azure-openai): OpenAI models hosted on Azure, such as GPT-5, GPT-4, o-series models
3. [Azure Foundry](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/anthropic-models-via-azure): models from providers such as OpenAI, Mistral, Meta, Cohere, DeepSeek, Phi
4. [Chutes AI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/chutes-ai): open-source models such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, GLM
5. [Fireworks AI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/fireworks-ai): open-source models such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mixtral
6. [Minimax](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/minimax): MiniMax models such as MiniMax-M1, MiniMax-Text, MiniMax-VL
7. [Hugging Face](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/hugging-face-models): open-source models such as Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma, DeepSeek
8. [AWS Bedrock Anthropic](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/aws-bedrock-anthropic): Claude models such as Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, Claude Haiku
9. [Ollama](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/ollama): local models such as Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma, DeepSeek
10. [LM Studio](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/lmstudio-\(phi-3-llama-3-falcon-gemma-gpt-neo-x\)): local models such as Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma, DeepSeek
11. [LocalAI](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/local-ai-\(llama-gpt4all-vicuna-falcon-etc.\)): local OpenAI-compatible models such as Llama, Mistral, Qwen
12. [Jina Deep Research](/manage-and-connect-ai-models/jina-deep-research): Jina DeepSearch / Deep Research models
